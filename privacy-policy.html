Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how mobile applications and related services published by Shin (collectively referred to as the "Apps", including CoinLens AI and utility applications) handle information, protect user privacy, and respect your rights.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all mobile applications developed and distributed by Shin on application distribution platforms such as Google Play Store and Apple App Store. By installing or using any of our Apps, you consent to the data handling practices described herein.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

We believe in privacy-first design. Whenever possible, our Apps store your personal content, preferences, and activity locally on your device:

  • User Collections & History: Catalog items, scan history, saved collections, favorites, and notes are stored strictly in your device's local database or private sandbox.
  • Application Preferences: Display preferences, unit measurements, currency selections, and onboarding status remain on your device.
  • This on-device information is not transmitted to our external servers without your explicit action or consent.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Depending on the specific App and features you use, the following permissions may be requested:

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: If an App provides scanning, optical inspection, cropping, or image analysis (such as specimen examination in CoinLens AI), camera access or photo library access is used exclusively on your device to capture or import images. We do not sell or secretly transmit your personal photos to remote databases.
  • Internet Access: Used to load online reference catalogs, high-resolution specimen images, database updates, or open external informational links.

4. Diagnostics, Crash Reporting & Analytics

To ensure high app reliability and monitor critical bugs, our Apps may integrate industry-standard diagnostic SDKs (such as Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics):

  • Diagnostic Data: In the event of a software crash or unexpected error, anonymized technical telemetry (device model, operating system version, error stack traces) may be logged to help our developers fix bugs.
  • Usage Telemetry: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics (such as screen view counts and feature engagement) may be processed to improve app ergonomics and responsiveness.
  • User Control: Production analytics and crash telemetry respect system-level privacy settings and in-app consent preferences where applicable.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Apps may reference external websites, educational databases (such as Numista or public auction archives), or leverage platform services provided by Google. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their terms when navigating external links.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • You can clear all app storage, cached images, and databases at any time through the in-app Settings menu or through Android / iOS System Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored application data and user preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age defined in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Apps, legal requirements, or platform policies. The latest version will always be accessible at this public URL with the effective date updated accordingly.
